Modulation links

The tree keeps the structure and the blend order. The modulation view holds the chains. Each chain has a colour, and every value it drives shows that colour. Click a row, a chain, a target or a source to follow a link.

Tree

Modulation

Properties

  1. Chain colour: the dots on a tree row and the ring on a value show which chains drive it, with no click.
  2. Selection in both directions: a chain lights its targets in the tree. A row lights the chains that drive it.
  3. Targets in the chain: each chain lists its targets with their object, so the view shows source to target.
  4. Wires on hover: only when the panels are side by side. Hover a chain or a row.
